## 2. Boilerplate vs vendor-specific

Across the 17 houses that publish anything (all but OBJECTS, Heritage, Prop Services West, and limits-wise Platinum):

Near-universal (safe to treat as the default packet)
- A certificate of insurance before pickup. Every studio-style house that publishes requirements asks for one: Gil & Roy, Omega, EC, History For Hire, Prop Heaven, Target, RC Vintage, Alley Cats, Universal, Pina, Alpha, Depict 33, ISS, Premiere, and WB "on request". The only published exceptions: HPR (COI waived if you leave a card deposit; required for net 30) and the two non-studio houses (Platinum, Art Dimensions) which never mention one.
- The COI wording is the same ACORD 25 "Description of Operations" boilerplate at most houses: the house is Certificate Holder, named Additional Insured for General Liability (and usually Auto), and Loss Payee for "Props, Sets, Wardrobe" (often "and Loss of Use, rental/leased to the named insured", often citing endorsement "form #CA9947"). Omega, Gil & Roy, Depict 33, Premiere, EC, Prop Heaven, History For Hire, Universal, ISS, Target and Pina all fit this pattern. Most also say the certificate must come from the production's insurance agent (not the production) and that the insured name must match the account/rental-agreement entity (Universal, EC, History For Hire).
- Validity window: "at least two weeks beyond the length of the rental" is repeated verbatim at Omega, History For Hire and Prop Heaven; EC says expiry no earlier than 7 days after the last return.
- Dollar limits, where published, are one of two stacks:
  - The "Omega stack" (Omega, Premiere): GL $1M occurrence / $2M aggregate / $100K fire legal / $5K med pay; Auto CSL $1M incl. hired & non-owned; Inland Marine / Producer's Package Props-Sets-Wardrobe $250K (lower acceptable if total production prop value is lower); Workers Comp.
  - The "Propcart/ISS stack" (ISS, Target, Pina): CGL $1M occurrence / $2M aggregate; all-risk property (rental floater or production package) not less than $1M at replacement cost; auto $1M CSL; workers comp with EL $1M; waiver of subrogation; primary & non-contributory. Target adds a $2M umbrella.
  - History For Hire and Universal publish only "GL minimum $1,000,000" (+ property at replacement value for Universal); Gil & Roy, EC, Depict 33, RC Vintage, Alley Cats, Prop Heaven, Alpha, WB publish wording but no dollar figures.
- Payment on or before pickup for anyone without an open account (Gil & Roy, Omega, EC, History For Hire, Prop Heaven, Depict 33, Premiere, HPR, Alpha, Universal).
- A two-tier account model: "COD / pay-at-pickup / single job" vs "PO / Net 30 / credit account". The credit tier always adds a credit application (or "credit one sheet"), references or a studio letter of guarantee, AP contact, PO policy, authorized users, and 7-10 working days (Omega, History For Hire, Prop Heaven, HPR, EC, ISS, Target, Pina, Art Dimensions, RC Vintage, Alley Cats, Gil & Roy, WB, Alpha).
- The house's own W-9 posted for download (Gil & Roy, History For Hire, Prop Heaven, RC Vintage, Alley Cats, WB, Depict 33, ISS, Art Dimensions, HPR), frequently with a CA Form 590 next to it. Nobody asks for the customer's W-9.

Credit card authorization, explicitly (who requires it, and for whom)
- Required of everyone paying by card, not tied to account status: Omega ("All orders paid by credit card must be accompanied by a credit card authorization form"; fax only, secured line, 3% surcharge), Platinum ("must be filled out for each event"), Gil & Roy (form posted, payment before pickup, 3% fee), Depict 33 (form posted, payment before pickup), Alpha ("Credit Card Authorization Sheet"; "All COD orders require a deposit ... NO EXCEPTIONS").
- Required for the no-account / COD / one-time tier only, and not listed for Net 30 / PO accounts: History For Hire (COD account = "Fill out and submit the Credit Card Authorization form"), Prop Heaven (COD cards need the authorization form), HPR (card deposit instead of a COI if you have no account), EC ("cc only rental deposit quick form" for single jobs; CC auth sent after the order is finalized), Target (COD: C.C.A. or C.C.D.A.; Net 30: not listed), Pina (COD and Student only), RC Vintage and Alley Cats (one-time rentals = CC auth + COI; Net 30 = credit one sheet instead), Premiere (CC auth form posted; first order prepaid; deposits in lieu of COI are cash/check only, no cards).
- No standalone CC authorization form published: Universal (card goes through the NBCU payment portal instead), ISS (COD handled by a "Deposit C.O.D. Account Agreement"), WB, Art Dimensions, Heritage, Prop Services West, OBJECTS.
- Deposits ride on the CC auth and are almost always an authorization hold, sized by insurance: full replacement value with no insurance (History For Hire, Prop Heaven, Premiere 10x rental, Omega 50%), reduced to the property-coverage deductible or 25% with a COI (Prop Heaven, History For Hire, Omega), or a floor ($2,500 at Universal).

Vendor-specific (only some houses)
- Signed rental agreement as a distinct document: Omega, History For Hire, Prop Heaven, EC (signed at pickup, no riders), Target/Pina (Propcart PDF), HPR (net 30), WB, Universal, ISS (web terms), Platinum (e-sign via Dropbox Sign/HelloSign). No wet-signature or notary requirement published anywhere. Not posted at Gil & Roy, Depict 33, RC Vintage, Alley Cats, Premiere, Art Dimensions.
- Photo ID at pickup: not published as a general rule by any house. It appears only for students (HPR, Omega, History For Hire, Pina) and for personal checks (Premiere).
- Business license / resale certificate: not asked for by anyone.
- Studio houses: Universal needs the insured to match the rental-agreement entity exactly, takes deposits only via its NBCU payment portal, and quotes a $2,500 deposit floor. WB routes Net 30 credit through the "WB Greenlight" portal by emailing WBSFAR@wbd.com and requires a separate Project Information Sheet web form; no CC auth is published.
- Crew/contact sheets naming the set decorator, lead, buyers, AP, PO contact: EC, Omega (Customer Contact Information / Billing Company forms), Alpha (Customer Information Sheet), Premiere ("List of authorized shoppers"), Prop Heaven and Omega (authorized account users).
- Studio letter of guarantee for Net 30: EC ("A letter of guarantee from a major studio"), History For Hire ("credit sheet and/or letter of guarantee").
- Fax-only or fax-preferred paperwork: Omega (CC auth "FAX this completed document to our SECURED FAX LINE"; COI to Credit Dept fax), Prop Heaven and Alley Cats offer fax as fallback.
- 3% credit card surcharge: Omega, Gil & Roy.
- Name-matching: the name on the card / check / COI must match the company on the invoice (EC, Universal).
- Platinum (events) is the outlier on prepayment: 100% of the rental to hold the date and a CC auth per event.

Order channel
- Takes orders/holds by email (address published): Gil & Roy (sales@), Omega (orderdesk@), EC (ecprops@aol.com), History For Hire (info@), Heritage (frank@/arnold@), Prop Services West (Sales@), Alley Cats (alleycatsprops@gmail.com), Depict 33 (info@), Premiere (corp@), Universal (universalprophouse@nbcuni.com), WB (WBSOProperty@wbd.com for the office; set lists live in the portal), Alpha (accounting@ for paperwork).
- Portal-first with a set list / hold request: Target, Pina, Art Dimensions (Propcart), ISS (props.issprops.com), WB (property.warnerbros.com Set Lists), Universal (props.universalstudios.com, bot-blocked), Alpha (admin.alphaprops.com), Prop Heaven (login + wish list), History For Hire (cart + register), Gil & Roy (Shopify cart submitted to sales), Platinum (Shopify).
- Phone-only published: RC Vintage ("speak to our manager Chris"), HPR, OBJECTS.
